AC Elite License / Safety Rating / How To

Your License (pace/skill) is based on your position on the leaderboards per track and your total km driven.

We do not use race results (wins or podiums)—only leaderboard pace and distance. So it works the same for sprint races and full grids, and a solo run never counts as a "win" for this system.

The server runs one track at a time (weekly rotation). "Tracks" below = number of different circuits you have set a time on over time.

We use our own safety rating system that calculates your overall safety on track. The formula takes into account how many incidents per distance you have (we use incidents per 100 km, similar to incidents per corner in a race).

The scale is from 1.00 (lowest) to 9.99 (highest). Every driver starts at a minimum rating of 2.50 until enough distance is driven. If you crash often and drive little, your rating stays low; if you finish many km cleanly, your incident-per-distance ratio improves and your rating goes up.

We weigh collisions (car/wall damage) and infractions (e.g. track cuts) per 100 km. The table on the right shows what Min SR and Min KM you need for each tier; you need both. 9.99 is theoretical (near-zero incidents); in practice, 2.5+ is already very clean, 3+ is excellent (S tier).
